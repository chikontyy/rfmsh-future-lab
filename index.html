What is RFMSH Future Lab?

RFMSH Future Lab is a personal research and design project created by a student of the Republican Physics and Mathematics School (RFMSH) — a school known for its strong focus on mathematics, physics and computer science.

The lab shows that technology has no borders: the interactive heart on this page is drawn with a mathematical curve, animated by a particle system and greets you in many languages of the world.
